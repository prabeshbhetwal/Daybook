# FocusContinuity product context

## Register

product

## Users

People working at a Mac who want to understand a whole working day, continue
interrupted focus, and distinguish intentional sessions from observed app use.
This is a native macOS tool for repeated daily use, not a web analytics dashboard.

## Product purpose

Keep time accounting honest across pauses, absence, screen locks and sleep.
Make the recorded day understandable without requiring users to reconstruct it
from disconnected charts. The menu bar provides quick operations; the main
window provides a chronological Story with Day, Week and Month scopes.

## Brand personality

Calm, precise, considered. Native macOS conventions and the supplied final
Day as a Story prototype are the approved references. Premium means predictable,
legible and reliable, not more decoration or more metrics.

## Anti-references

- Empty dashboard cards, repeated labels and information without a clear scope.
- Unattended time presented as observed work, or invented claims about productivity.
- Navigation that sends historical evidence to an unrelated current-day screen.
- Decorative controls that store a value without changing the interface.
- Oversized imitation sheets without native keyboard or focus behaviour.

## Design principles

1. Evidence precedes interpretation. Keep recording limitations beside the figures.
2. A change of scope never changes a measure's definition.
3. Reveal detail where the user asks for it; preserve their reading context.
4. Corrections are explicit, durable and reversible. Source app-use history is preserved.
5. Use native conventions for windowing, menus, focus, keyboard operation and appearance.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Support macOS 13, VoiceOver, keyboard-only operation, System appearance, light and
dark themes, reduced motion and readable normal-text contrast. Colour supplements
literal dates, values, selection state and labels. Copy uses Australian English.
