B Day as a story
One column, one narrow measure, read top to bottom like a page. A time rail runs down the left and every session, break and gap is an entry on it — including the gaps, which are entries too. No charts on this screen: numbers live in the header, and periods are a scope control above the column. Calmest of the three, and the one that makes an unrecorded hour feel like an honest part of the day rather than a defect.
