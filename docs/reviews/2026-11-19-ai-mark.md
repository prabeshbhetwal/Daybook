# Design review: the AI mark, the suggestion and the Settings row

Phase C Task 5, ruling C17. Reviewed 20 November 2026 from the `--snapshot`
images in `docs/screenshots/2026-11-20-ai-mark/` (light and dark), taken at
commit 704e809.

## What was reviewed

- `storyAI-compact-light.png`, `storyAI-compact-dark.png`: three session rows
  (two carry the mark) and three reports: an AI pick, an AI-made label, and a
  shadow-mode suggestion.
- `settingsData-comfortable-light.png`, `-dark.png`,
  `settingsData-minimum-light.png`, `-dark.png`: the Privacy group with the
  new switch and the tally line.

## What works

- The row mark sits between the category chip and the time, in supporting ink
  at caption size. It is quiet enough not to compete with the category and
  reads in both appearances.
- The report's AI row sits where the remembered-correction row sits, so the
  person meets one place for "Daybook guessed this, check it". Accepted in
  place of the header (implementer deviation 2).
- The sentence states what happened in plain words ("On-device AI filed this
  under Deep work.", "On-device AI made the category Films for this.") and the
  actions follow it in reading order.
- The Settings switch sits under "Use on-device AI", which it depends on, and
  the tally line under it is plain and countable.
- "On-device AI" in place of "Apple Intelligence" matches the existing switch
  and the build's rule against the trademark in source strings. C17's help
  text becomes "Assigned by on-device AI. Open the session to keep or change
  it." (implementer deviation 1).

## Changes required

1. **Menu buttons do not match the link actions.** "Change…" and
   "Merge Into" render in primary ink with a chevron while Keep, Rename… and
   Remove are accent links, so the row reads as two kinds of control. Give the
   two menus the same accent ink and weight as the links, keeping the chevron.
2. **The suggestion does not say who suggests it.** "New category: Writing"
   with Use gives no attribution, unlike the two filed states and against C21
   (nothing presented as the person's own or as fact). Copy: "On-device AI
   suggests a new category, Writing." for a proposed label, and "On-device AI
   suggests Deep work." for a pick or an existing label. Use stays.
3. **The switch caption reads as a fragment.** "Off, its choice shows only as
   a suggestion in the session report." Copy: "While off, the AI's choice
   shows only as a suggestion in the session report." It describes shadow mode
   honestly whether the switch is on or off.

## Not changed

- The light-mode "Use on-device AI" switch tint predates this task.
- The full privacy wording (what the model sees and never sees, the label
  file) belongs to the Settings help and README in Task 6 (C21).

## Verdict

Ship after changes 1 to 3, with new snapshots to confirm them.
