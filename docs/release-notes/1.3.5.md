## Daybook 1.3.5

Daily goals now earn awards, you can limit a whole kind of activity, and a session can be split in two. This update also fixes several crashes, and carries forward what 1.3.4 shipped but never announced.

### 1. New in 1.3.5

**Goals and awards**
- **Perfect day and goal awards.** Today's goals now earn a Perfect day when every goal is kept, and individual goals earn awards.
- **Awards stay.** An award a goal has earned stays after the goal is paused or deleted.
- **Fairer judging.** A closed day is judged by its own goals, and only when something was recorded.
- **A fortnight of results.** Goal results reach back two weeks and are written after the day's evidence settles.
- **Yesterday notice.** It now learns of a perfect day once its results are recorded. Until then, Today's goals say so themselves.
- **Daily limits on a kind of activity.** You can set a limit on an activity such as Media or Communication.
- **Honest limits.** A limit on a kind of site shows "not available yet" instead of reading as kept, and a day with a limit that cannot be judged is no longer called perfect.

**Sessions**
- **Split and merge.** A session can be split in two and merged back without changing your totals.
- **Split suggestions.** Daybook offers to split a session that held two pieces of work. Automatic sessions split themselves, with Merge back.
- **How a session began.** Each session shows quietly how it began, and where its category came from. The report notes when the activity contradicts the label.
- **Remembered categories.** Daybook remembers how you file a kind of session and suggests it next time. Undoing a change also forgets the suggestion.
- **Away answers.** Sessions you change after an away answer keep how they began.

**Browsers and meetings**
- Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi and other browsers are now counted as browsers.
- A meeting held in a browser is not flagged as mostly video.

**Settings and privacy**
- Settings shows an opt-in site signal. It stays unavailable until it has been tested on your Mac.
- The privacy notes now describe how sessions record their origin and remembered categories.
- Today's goals and Time of day no longer repeat "Today" in their headers.

### 2. Fixes

1. A damaged summary inside one part of a session no longer crashes its report.
2. Damaged evidence files can no longer crash a report or erase remembered sites.
3. A day with every goal paused no longer breaks a goal's run.
4. Undoing category choices in any order keeps the ones still standing.
5. Undoing an old category choice keeps the newer one, and a part split again merges back in order.
6. Merging a split session back keeps its whole activity summary.
7. A category choice is remembered once, even when saving it is retried.
8. Splitting or merging a session survives an interrupted save, and retrying finishes what it started.

### 3. Missed in 1.3.4

These shipped in 1.3.4 without a mention in its update window:

- **Today's goals.** The daily goal card is now Today's goals, and several goals can be stored. Each category goal counts the same way the ring does.
- **App limits.** An app can have a daily limit, shown on Today's goals. They show only on today, and say so when app recording is off. An app limit cannot be added twice.
- **What your time was spent on.** Each session shows its time phase by phase. Breaks between stretches are kept, manual pauses are respected, and unrecorded time never borrows a label.
- **Steadier running strip.** A running session's strip no longer flips between its two readings every half-minute.
- **Polish.** The green window button snaps back level with the others, the menu bar panel's buttons share one size, and Done on the Yesterday card has a clear blue fill.
- **Safer goals.** A full goal list or a category reset no longer leaves a goal that vanishes, and a paused category goal survives a rename.
